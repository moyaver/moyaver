## Mohammad Jaber

**Android Engineer · Fintech, Payments & Mobile Security**

5+ years building consumer-facing Android apps in Kotlin, with a focus on modern architecture (Compose, Coroutines/Flow, MVVM, Clean Architecture) and mobile security aligned with OWASP MASVS.

Currently at **Neo Mena Technologies** on banking infrastructure. Previously shipped payments (MontyPay) and eSIM products at **Monty Mobile**.

### What I work on

- **Mobile security** — OWASP MASVS-aligned hardening, SSL pinning, Play Integrity, keystore work, R8 obfuscation
- **Modern Android** — Jetpack Compose, Coroutines/Flow, MVVM, Clean Architecture, modularization
- **Cross-platform** — Kotlin Multiplatform for sharing business logic across Android and iOS
- **Offensive security** — TryHackMe pentesting and reverse engineering, to better understand the threat side of the apps I ship
